<p>Watchdog is a multidisciplinary team of journalists, researchers and software engineers, operating under the Appendix umbrella. We hunt misinformation, investigate matters of public welfare, and build software tools.</p>
<p>We began in April 2019, days after the Easter Sunday bombings in Sri Lanka, as a group of concerned citizens trying to counter misinformation. We did this amidst <a href="https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/janelytvynenko/sri-lanka-social-media-block">government crackdowns on freedom of expression</a> and <a href="https://slate.com/technology/2019/04/sri-lanka-social-media-block-disinformation.html">mass hysteria caused by fake news and rumors</a>, building a mobile app that was used by over 200,000 people to verify information and counteract rumors in their own networks.</p>
<p><img src="https://sgp1.digitaloceanspaces.com/watchdog-public-assets/image%20%2811%29.png" loading="lazy" alt="Image"></p>
<h2>How we work</h2>
<p>We've been called an <a href="https://restofworld.org/2022/south-asia-newsletter-sri-lanka-ride-hailing/">“open-source intelligence research collective”</a>, and that's fairly accurate. We use a lot of OSINT techniques - a combination of data scraping, analysis of publicly available documents and datasets, paired with old-school boots-on-the-ground journalism.</p>
<p>As a core part of Appendix, we also design and act as a testing ground for new technology. This includes work like the #AskWatchdog AI and various parts of our data scraping, information extraction and archive operations.</p>

<p>These are our <a href="/operational-ethics">Operational ethics</a>. Here’s how we go about <a href="/handling-conflicts">Handling conflict</a> - whether that’s related to a story we’re working on or an internal issue. Lastly, we’re known for our no-bullshit style (which tends to annoy people who expect flowery abstractions and vagueness), so here’s our <a href="/language-guide">Language guide.</a></p>

<h2>Who we are</h2>
<p>The original incarnation of Watchdog was staffed by volunteer factcheckers and translators. We’ve grown significantly since then. Many of our team have strong journalism and research backgrounds, with a sprinkling of computer science to make sure we have the tools to keep up with our ambitions.</p>
